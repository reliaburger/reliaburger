# 0.2.1 release closure

17–21 October 2026. How the 0.2.1 candidates were qualified: what each
candidate failed on, why, and the fix that went into the next one. The
per-run evidence lives in the records this page links; this page is the map.
The gate issue is [#674](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/674).

> **Candidate 9 (`abec2c45`) is the release candidate.** It passed both staged
> installs, the V02 fast tier and the jobs fast tier. Its first 8-hour jobs
> final failed only on one slow agent-loop turn during a host disk stall
> ([#1255](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1255),
> [below](#the-8-hour-jobs-final)), and the maintainer had the final re-run on
> the same candidate. Run 2 completed all 8 cycles. Its only failures came
> from two slow agent-loop turns, both fsync'd discovery journal writes on the
> loop. One was #1255 again, which the checker counted twice
> ([#1261](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1261)). The other
> came from the same cause at a second call site
> ([#1266](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1266)). The
> maintainer accepted both issues as known for 0.2.1, with the fixes in 0.2.2.
>
> The power-cut and reboot lanes then passed 25 of 25 in every lane, which
> completes the gate.
>
> **Known issue it ships with:** after the gate, RustSec published
> [RUSTSEC-2026-0381, -0382 and -0383](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1269)
> (one CVE). The locked rustls-webpki and webpki-roots trust the distrusted
> emSign intermediates, whose private keys are public, on outbound HTTPS:
> image pulls, object storage and other public endpoints. Cluster mTLS uses
> the cluster's own CA and isn't affected. 0.2.0 has the same versions. The
> maintainer decided (21 October 2026) to ship 0.2.1 from candidate 9 rather
> than build and qualify a new candidate with the upgrade. `main` has the
> patched versions, and 0.2.2 is the first release built with them
> ([#1269](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1269)).

## The gate

0.2.1 is the first release on the shorter gate
([#991](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/991), maintainer
decision, 17 October 2026): two staged installs, the V02 fast tier, the jobs
fast tier, then **one** 8-hour jobs final
(`qualify-jobs-sustained.sh --tier final`) and the power-cut and reboot lanes.
The jobs final is the V02 soak with the job campaign and a node fault in every
cycle, so it checks everything the V02 final does, plus the jobs. It replaces
0.2.0's separate 8-hour V02 final and 12-hour jobs final.

The lanes ran 25 times each rather than 10, as they did for 0.2.0, because
0.2.1 changes both paths they cover. It upgrades redb to 4.x so that a power
cut while the Raft store grows can't leave it unopenable
([#1024](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1024),
[#1028](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1028)). It also
changes `relish local stop` to power VMs off from inside rather than cutting
their power ([#1023](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1023),
[#1025](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1025)). Zero failures
in 25 runs bounds each lane's failure rate below 12% at 95% confidence. Ten
would only bound it below 30%.

## The 8-hour jobs final

### Run 1

Run 1 ran from 03:03 to 11:05 UTC on 20 October and completed all 8 cycles. It
recorded one failure. At 19:00:59 +08 (11:00:59 UTC), with no fault active,
all three VMs hit the same disk stall from the host. Nodes 1 and 2 were in
consumer-sync journal writes, which stop waiting at their 500 ms turn
deadline and leave the write in flight. Node 3 was publishing a health-probe
result, which journals with no deadline:

```
19:00:59 node-1: agent loop turn took 500 ms in follow_up (consumer_sync/journal): journal write 500 ms
19:00:59 node-2: agent loop turn took 500 ms in follow_up (consumer_sync/journal): journal write 500 ms
19:01:02 node-3: agent loop turn took 3950 ms in deploy_op (health_probe_result): journal write 3950 ms
```

So the checker failed the tier at the next settle (11:01 UTC):
`agent-loop-turn rb-…-3: 1 deploy_op turn(s) over the 1 s budget, the worst
between 2.5 and 5 s`. That's [#1255](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1255),
in 0.2.2: the health-probe publication should get the same turn deadline.
[#1100](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1100) (candidate 5)
stopped it waiting for an earlier write in flight; this is its own write. The
maintainer classified run 1 as a host artefact and re-ran the final on the
same candidate.

Everything else in run 1 passed:

- **Throughput:** 3,840 accepted a second against the 1,390 target: 81.5
  million process, 28.6 million shared-runc and 64,264 runc successes.
- **Memory:** the worst Bun was node 2's, at 203.87 MiB steady and 207.34 MiB
  peak, against 384 MiB (512 MiB on node 1, which serves the API).
- **Batch wall time:** 64 arrays of 1,048,576 tasks, 360 s at the median and
  1,499 s at worst, against a 600 s target that's recorded, not gated.
- **Renewals and rotations:** 17 workload identity rotations and 17 node leaf
  renewals per node, 91 ingress reloads, and the job campaign drained with
  nothing missing.

### Run 2

Run 2 ran from 12:03 to 20:05 UTC on 20 October and completed all 8 cycles
([record](2026-10-21-v0.2.1-job-qualification.md)). It recorded three
failures, all `agent-loop-turn`:

- **Failure 1** (heavy check, 15:29 UTC): during upgrade walk 2, with no fault
  on that node, node 2 logged
  `23:28:31 bun[676]: agent loop turn took 1289 ms in deploy_op (health_probe_result)`.
  That's #1255 again, so it isn't only a one-off host stall.
- **Failure 2** (settle check, 16:01:35 UTC, after the graceful whole-cluster
  stop and start): the same turn, counted twice. Node 2's full journal has only
  that one slow turn; Bun then restarted (pid 676 to 730) and the new process
  logged no turn over 1 s. The checker resets its baseline when the process
  changes, but a branch the new process hasn't reported yet still resolves to
  the old process's counts
  ([#1261](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1261), a harness
  bug, in 0.2.2).

Maintainer decision (21 October 2026): for 0.2.1, `agent-loop-turn` failures
from #1255 are a known issue, accepted provided every other check passes. The
fix ships in 0.2.2.

- **Failure 3** (settle check, 20:01:27 UTC, cycle 8): node 1 logged
  `04:00:41 bun[2934972]: agent loop turn took 2493 ms in deploy_op (apply_network_pre_start)`.
  The node's disk had been slow for minutes: "a discovery journal write took
  1444 ms" at 03:53 and "1632 ms" at 03:56. Starting a workload journals its
  retained network reference
  (`retain_network_reference` → `persist_discovery_reference`) with no turn
  deadline, so it waited on the loop for the write already in flight and then
  for its own. It's the #1255 cause at a second call site, filed as
  [#1266](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1266) for 0.2.2.
  The record's data section counts two slow turns on node 1. The turn
  histograms show the second is the #1261 double count again: node 1 had none
  over 1 s at 20:00:02 UTC, and none after it restarted at the final graceful
  stop.

Maintainer decisions (21 October 2026): for 0.2.1, `agent-loop-turn` failures
from #1255 and #1266 are known issues, accepted provided every other check
passes. Both fixes ship in 0.2.2.

Under those decisions, run 2 passes. Everything else passed:

- **Faults:** every fault, special, upgrade walk and rollback settled, with
  none failed or skipped. The slowest was the quorum loss, at 65 s.
- **Throughput:** 3,764 accepted a second against the 1,390 target: 79.9
  million process, 28.0 million shared-runc and 63,675 runc successes.
- **Memory:** the worst Bun was node 2's, at 203.81 MiB steady and 208.88 MiB
  peak, against 384 MiB (512 MiB on node 1).
- **Batch wall time:** 64 arrays of 1,048,576 tasks, 381 s at the median and
  1,398 s at worst, against a 600 s target that's recorded, not gated.
- **Renewals and rotations:** 17 workload identity rotations and 17 node leaf
  renewals per node, 91 ingress reloads, and the job campaign drained with
  nothing missing.
- **Data:** the volume writer reached ACK 240,228 and the Redis counter 26,409,
  with no writer-lost or writer-regression failure. Nine registry images were
  pushed and verified again.

## Known issues shipped in 0.2.1

- A health-probe publication journals with no turn deadline, so a slow disk
  can hold the agent loop for seconds
  ([#1255](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1255), 0.2.2).
  The soak saw turns of 1.3 s and 3.95 s. Nothing was lost, but every caller
  on that node waits for the turn.
- 0.2.1 is the last release with an Intel macOS build (`relish-macos-x86_64`).
  It's a CLI-only build that no staged install or soak has qualified; 0.2.2
  drops it ([#1018](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1018)).
