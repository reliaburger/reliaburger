# 0.2.6 release closure

5–6 November 2026. How the 0.2.6 candidate was qualified: what each gate run
found, and what's left for after the tag. The per-run evidence lives in the
records this page links; this page is the map.

> **Candidate 2 (`50596cfe`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane)
> and the V02 and jobs fast tiers, both clean with no agent-loop turn over
> budget. The 8-hour jobs final completed its whole schedule: 8 cycles, four
> upgrade walks and a 2¼-hour quiet tail with every leak checker armed on
> every node. It recorded two failures, and every other check passed:
>
> 1. a `chaos-partition` fault on node 1 refused at 15:54 GMT, because the
>    source instance's cgroup lookup missed the inject turn's deadline
>    ([#1601](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1601),
>    [below](#a-refused-partition-fault));
> 2. one `follow_up` turn on node 1 of 1,232 ms, against the agent loop's
>    1 s budget, at 01:20 GMT in the quiet tail
>    ([#1606](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1606),
>    [below](#one-slow-compaction-turn-on-node-1)).
>
> The maintainer accepted both and released 0.2.6 with them as known issues:
> the first on 5 November 2026 while the final was running, the second on
> 6 November 2026 when it appeared. That's a decision, not the
> harness-artefact rule: by the rule of
> [releasing.md](../releasing.md#staging-a-candidate) the final is not clean.
> Both are fixed or tracked in 0.2.7.
>
> **Known issues it ships with:** #1601 and #1606, and the 13 RustSec
> advisories against `zstd` 0.13.3 and `zstd-safe` 7.3.0 that 0.2.3 to 0.2.5
> shipped with ([record](2026-10-26-dependency-exceptions.md)), whose
> exception expires on 18 November 2026.

## The gate

0.2.6 needs the full gate ([a gate scaled to the
release](../releasing.md#a-gate-scaled-to-the-release)): it moves `CURRENT`
(protocol 56 → 57,
[#1548](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1548)) and changes
the agent loop. `scripts/release/nightly.py freeze-check` found no green
nightly to shorten it. That's two staged installs, the V02 fast tier, the
jobs fast tier, one 8-hour jobs final and the power-cut and reboot lanes, 25
runs each, all on the candidate itself.

It is the first gate on the Mac Studio. The MacBook Pro that ran 0.2.5's
gate has run jobs at about a quarter of its old rate since 2 November
([0.2.5's closure](2026-11-03-v0.2.5-release-closure.md#a-quarter-of-the-job-throughput)).
An A/B run on the Studio before the gate showed that release builds in the
background, even at background priority, cost a slow turn, so nothing else
ran on the Studio while the gate did.

## A refused partition fault

At 15:54 GMT, 42 minutes into the final, the soak asked node 1 for a
partition fault whose source was `soak-redis-client`, and Bun refused it:

```
fault rejected: no running instance of source app soak-redis-client on this node has a verified workload cgroup
```

The instance was running on node 1. Node 1's journal shows the inject turn
hitting its deadline ("agent loop turn took 500 ms in command
(inject_fault)"). `check_connect_fault` in `src/bun/agent/faults.rs` finds
the source's callers through `local_callers()`, whose cgroup lookup is
bounded by the turn's deadline; under load the lookup timed out, the caller
went pending with no cgroup, and the fault had no verified source. Refusing
it is the safe way to fail: Bun never installs a partition it can't scope to
the workload. The next partition, at 21:54, was injected and settled in
17 s.

The maintainer decided on 5 November 2026 to release with it if it stayed
the final's only failure.
[#1603](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1603) fixes it on
the 0.2.7 merge train: a source whose cgroup lookup is only late is accepted
once the lookup lands.

## One slow compaction turn on node 1

At 01:23 GMT, two hours into the quiet tail with no faults injected, a heavy
check found one turn on node 1 over the 1 s budget:

```
bun: agent loop turn took 1232 ms in follow_up (consumer_sync/compact): journal write 1232 ms
```

Around it, every other consumer step on all three nodes stops at the turn
deadline, about 500 ms, and hands its discovery journal write to the
background ("a discovery journal write took 1098 ms; the agent loop went on
meanwhile"). This compaction step waited for its whole write inside the
turn instead. Nodes 2 and 3 had no turn over budget in the whole run, and
the host was quiet: 62% idle, no swap, nothing else running.

The maintainer accepted it on 6 November 2026 rather than build a third
candidate and run the gate again.
[#1606](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1606) tracks the
fix in 0.2.7. The evidence for both failures is in the final's
`failures/1/` and `failures/2/` directories: the three nodes' journals,
diagnostics and the cluster state.
