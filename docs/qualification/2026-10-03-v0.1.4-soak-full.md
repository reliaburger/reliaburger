## Disposition (3 October 2026)

Neither failure followed a fault: the last fault had settled 7–8 minutes before each pulse. Neither is a 0.1.4 regression. `src/sesame`, the registry routes, Pickle's request path and disk-pressure resignation are unchanged since v0.1.3, and 0.1.4's new `src/pickle/binding.rs` isn't called yet.

1. **Registry 503s (16:14), #509.** The soak's one-hour leaves: node 1's leaf issued at 15:11:01 expired at 16:11:01, although it had been renewed. Pickle's pooled connection to the registry leader never went idle, so it kept presenting the old leaf. From 16:11:02 the leader refused it with 403 (`registry leader refused proposal: 403 Forbidden`, about 130 times), which Pickle's clients saw as 503.
2. **No leader (17:12), #510.** Node 1, the leader, held more unexported logs and metrics than the soak's `max_storage_mb = 8` on three ticks in a row. That counted as sustained disk pressure, so it resigned (`17:11:50 n1 bun: sustained disk pressure — advertising council resignation`), although its disk was 38% full. Seven lease requests at 17:11:51 found no leader. The cluster was healthy at the 17:12:36 settle check.

The maintainer ran the final 8 h tier and the upgrade walks with the release key, and decided to release 0.1.4 with #505, #509 and #510 as known issues, to be fixed in 0.1.5.
