## Disposition (3 October 2026)

Both failures are the same finding: every over-budget turn was `sync_cluster_consumer`, on all three nodes. A sync that changed the view wrote three to five fsync'd discovery-journal records in one turn. At about 400 ms a write on the hosted runner's shared disk, those turns reached 1.0–1.2 s around the first leader kill. Not a 0.1.4 regression: `src/bun/agent/consumer.rs` and its handler are unchanged since v0.1.3. Tracked as #505 (0.1.5), fixed in #506 (one journal write a loop turn). The maintainer decided to release 0.1.4 with it as a known issue.
