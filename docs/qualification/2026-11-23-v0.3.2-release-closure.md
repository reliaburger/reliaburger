# 0.3.2 release closure

23 November 2026. How the 0.3.2 candidate was qualified: what each gate run
found, and what follows from it. The per-run evidence lives in the records
this page links; this page is the map.

> **Candidate 1 (`2aae7e2a`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane),
> the V02 fast tier, the jobs fast tier and the 8-hour jobs final, all clean
> and all at the first attempt. The final ran its whole schedule (10 h
> 15 min: eight cycles, four upgrade walks, both quorum losses, the all-off
> and a quiet tail with every leak checker armed on every node, all ok;
> every fault class settled every time; council history linearizable; no
> data lost) with no failures.
>
> **Known issues accepted at this gate:** none. 0.3.1's #2197 is fixed in
> this release (#2198), and chaos pause and chaos drop settled in the final
> in 23 s and 70 s. #2128, 0.3.0's registry deploy stall, didn't recur (the
> catalogue pulse passed all eight runs), but its cause is still unproven
> and the issue stays open.

## The gate

`scripts/release/release_tier.py --previous v0.3.1 --release v0.3.2` asked
for the full gate, for two reasons. 0.3.2 moves the wire protocol from 60 to
61 (#2157: an apply error carries its HTTP status), so it isn't
rolling-compatible with 0.3.1; the durable state stays at 76. It also
changes the agent loop (`src/bun/agent*`: #2157's apply status and the
terminal UI's node page, #2170), and any change there gets the 8-hour jobs
final. Every tier ran on the candidate itself, on the Mac Studio, from
01:01 to 14:42 GMT, with nothing else built or tested there while it ran.

| Gate | Result | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Power cut and reboot | PASS, 25 of 25 in every lane | [summary](2026-11-23-power-cut-reboot.md) |
| Staged install, Apple silicon | PASS, 134 s to a ready cluster | [record](2026-11-23-v0.3.2-staged-install-apple-silicon.md) |
| Staged install, Apple silicon (2) | PASS, 66 s | [record](2026-11-23-v0.3.2-staged-install-apple-silicon-2.md) |
| V02 fast tier | PASS, 1 h 08 min of soak | [record](2026-11-23-v0.3.2-sustained-v02-fast.md) |
| Jobs fast tier | PASS, 1 h 06 min of soak | [record](2026-11-23-v0.3.2-jobs-fast.md) |
| 8-hour jobs final | PASS, 10 h 15 min of soak | [record](2026-11-23-v0.3.2-jobs-final.md) |

The first staged install took 134 s because one of its three VMs didn't
boot, and the installer restarted it (70 s for that VM against 12 to 14 s
for the others); the second took 66 s.

The final's slowest settle was 105 s, after an upgrade walk. One agent-loop
turn on node 1 ran 322 ms past its deadline at 11:49 (822 ms in `snapshot`),
during the upgrade walk to the soak build: the loop's thread was waiting on
a page from disk while other workers sat in `fdatasync` and `fsync`. The
stall watchdog logged it and the loop went on. It isn't a gated check, and
no turn went over the 1 s budget on any node.
