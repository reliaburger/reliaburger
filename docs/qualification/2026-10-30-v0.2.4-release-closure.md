# 0.2.4 release closure

29–30 October 2026. How the 0.2.4 candidate was qualified: what each gate run
found, what changed in the harness on the way, and what's left for after the
tag. The per-run evidence lives in the records this page links; this page is
the map.

> **Candidate 1 (`964b56a2`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane),
> the V02 and jobs fast tiers and the 8-hour jobs final. The final ran 10 h
> 16 min: 8 cycles of faults and a 2¼-hour quiet tail with every leak
> checker armed on every node, at 6,899.6 accepted jobs a second against the
> 1,390 target. Its job-start p99 was 32 ms over 137 million quiet tasks,
> against the 256 ms SLO that 0.2.4 is the first release to gate on.
>
> **Both fast tiers first failed on a harness bug**, not the candidate. The
> job-start SLO had judged each of them on 20 samples
> ([#1485](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1485),
> [below](#the-job-start-slo-on-20-samples)). With the fixed checker
> ([#1486](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1486)), replayed
> on the same evidence, both pass; the job-start row and its note are the
> only lines that change.
>
> **Known issue it ships with:** the 13 RustSec advisories against `zstd`
> 0.13.3 and `zstd-safe` 7.3.0 that 0.2.3 shipped with
> ([record](2026-10-26-dependency-exceptions.md)). The exception expires on
> 18 November 2026; the fix,
> [#1366](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1366), moved to
> 0.3.0. `make audit` on the candidate's `Cargo.lock` against the advisory
> database of 30 October 2026 found nothing new.

## The gate

0.2.4 needs the full gate ([a gate scaled to the
release](../releasing.md#a-gate-scaled-to-the-release)): it moves `CURRENT`
(protocol 53 → 55, state format 70 → 72) and changes the agent loop. That's
two staged installs, the V02 fast tier, the jobs fast tier, one 8-hour jobs
final and the power-cut and reboot lanes, 25 runs each. The soaks and lanes
ran on the candidate itself.

The profile ([#1358](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1358))
and the memory run for
[#1417](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1417) and
[#1403](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1403) are
[performance records after the
tag](../releasing.md#performance-records-after-the-tag) and don't block it.

## The job-start SLO on 20 samples

0.2.4 gates the soak on a latency SLO for the first time
([#1400](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1400)): a job
task's start lag on a pooled executor, p99 ≤ 256 ms. The merge train's
review found that it counted runs during faults
([#1438](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1438)), and the
fix counted only runs that no fault window overlapped. In a fast tier the
faults come back to back, so almost nothing qualified:

| Run | Start-lag samples | Quiet samples | p99, all | p99, quiet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V02 fast | 141,530 | 20 | 2 ms | 2,048 ms |
| jobs fast | 15,665,402 | 20 | 32 ms | 2,048 ms |
| jobs final | | 137,306,923 | | 32 ms |

The p99 of 20 samples is their largest, and in both fast tiers 4 of the 20
took between 1 and 2 s. Every other check passed. Now the gate wants at
least 1,000 quiet samples before it judges the p99: a fast tier short of them
records the value as too few to judge, and a final, whose quiet tail gives it
millions, fails
([#1485](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1485),
[#1486](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1486)). Both fast
records were rendered again from their evidence with that checker. That's
the pre-1.0 rule for known harness artefacts (step 4 of [staging a
candidate](../releasing.md#staging-a-candidate)): it's written for a final,
and it's applied here to the fast tiers on the same terms. Nothing failed but
the checker's verdict, and no data was lost. The records as run are kept in
this page's directory. The final, with the original
checker, judged the SLO on 137 million samples and passed.

## Harness changes during the gate

Every change was to the harness, not the candidate, so the candidate wasn't
rebuilt.

- **Python 3.11.** The harness imports `tomllib`, which is Python 3.11 and
  later, and the qualification Mac has Python 3.9. The first attempt at every
  soak failed at its first TOML read. The gate then ran with `tomli` 2.5.0
  standing in for `tomllib` on `PYTHONPATH`
  ([#1483](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1483) makes
  the requirement explicit).
- **Policies in their own manifest.** The API refuses policies in a manifest
  that has jobs, and 0.2.4's soak put both to the workloads manifest, so
  the next attempts failed at the first apply. A first fix moved the jobs out
  instead, which broke the jobs' `run_before` hooks, which must name an app
  in the same apply. The soaks that passed apply the policies first, then the
  apps with their jobs
  ([#1484](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1484)), from that
  PR's branch; it's on main now.
- **Ports left behind.** Stopping a jobs fast run during its bootstrap, to
  pick up that fix, left its VMs holding the cluster's host ports, and the
  next V02 fast failed its install on them. The VMs were deleted and the gate
  started again.

None of those attempts reached a fault: each stopped before or at its first
apply or install.

## One process's memory, in the final

The final's worst Bun was on node 1: 472.4 MiB steady, under its 512 MiB
target. Every other node-1 process stayed between 189 and 213 MiB. That one
started at 21:44 GMT, when the soak killed node 1's Bun with `SIGKILL`. Within
90 s the new process had 406 MiB of anonymous memory, and it held that, flat,
until the upgrade walk at 22:05 exec'd it. After the exec it ran at 178 MiB
and stayed about there until 22:34, when a slow-fsync fault on node 1 took it
to 316 MiB in two minutes; the all-off special at 22:38 restarted it. So it
wasn't steady growth: a restart's catch-up, and then a slow disk, each
allocated a burst, and the allocator kept it. That's the shape
[#1417](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1417)'s findings
describe for the 0.2.3 day run, and the memory run after the tag measures it.
`leak-rss` passed on every node.
