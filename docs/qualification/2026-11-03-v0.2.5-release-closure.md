# 0.2.5 release closure

1–3 November 2026. How the 0.2.5 candidate was qualified: what each gate run
found, and what's left for after the tag. The per-run evidence lives in the
records this page links; this page is the map.

> **Candidate 1 (`954bb7b1`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane)
> and the V02 and jobs fast tiers. The 8-hour jobs final completed its whole
> schedule, 8 cycles and a 2¼-hour quiet tail with every leak checker armed on
> every node, and every check passed but one: a single `health_tick` turn on
> node 3 took 1,077 ms, against the agent loop's 1 s budget.
> The maintainer accepted that turn as a host stall on 3 November 2026
> ([below](#one-slow-turn-on-node-3)). That's a decision, not the harness-artefact
> rule: by the rule of [releasing.md](../releasing.md#staging-a-candidate)
> the final is not clean.
>
> **The final ran on a hot host.** The Mac was plugged in at 10% charge two
> minutes before the final started, and macOS logged thermal emergencies at
> 21:24, 00:23 and 04:24 GMT. Job throughput fell to a quarter of 0.2.4's
> from 21:24 on, and the slow turn came four minutes after the second
> ([below](#a-quarter-of-the-job-throughput)). The jobs fast tier on the same
> candidate ran at 0.2.4's rate.
>
> **Known issues it ships with:** the 13 RustSec advisories against `zstd`
> 0.13.3 and `zstd-safe` 7.3.0 that 0.2.3 and 0.2.4 shipped with
> ([record](2026-10-26-dependency-exceptions.md)), whose exception expires
> on 18 November 2026, and
> [RUSTSEC-2026-0433](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0433.html)
> in `pulldown-cmark` 0.13.5 ([below](#rustsec-2026-0433)).

## The gate

0.2.5 needs the full gate ([a gate scaled to the
release](../releasing.md#a-gate-scaled-to-the-release)): it moves `CURRENT`
(protocol 55 → 56, state format 72 → 73,
[#1512](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1512)) and changes
the agent loop ([#1513](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1513)).
That's two staged installs, the V02 fast tier, the jobs fast tier, one 8-hour
jobs final and the power-cut and reboot lanes, 25 runs each, all on the
candidate itself. The gate was split around a workshop on 2 November: the
installs, lanes and fast tiers ran overnight on 1–2 November, and the final
on the night of 2–3 November.

The 12-hour memory run for
[#1417](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1417) is a
[performance record after the
tag](../releasing.md#performance-records-after-the-tag) and doesn't block it.

## One slow turn on node 3

The final's only failure was a heavy check at 00:29 GMT: one `health_tick`
turn on node 3 over the 1 s budget. The soak had killed node 3's Bun with
`SIGKILL` at 00:22:51, as a scheduled `bun-kill-follower` fault, and the new
process logged the turn at 00:27:09:

```
bun: agent loop turn took 1077 ms in health_tick
```

The log line names no phase. Bun breaks a slow turn down by the work it timed
(journal writes, the runtime inventory, the kernel lock, the firewall sync),
and none of them was slow enough to list, so the time went somewhere the
turn didn't measure. Around it, node 3's journal writes were taking 250 to
270 ms, against single milliseconds before, and the registry leader was
refusing proposals with 503 while the cluster settled. Nodes 1 and 2 had no
turn over budget in the whole run.

macOS logged a thermal emergency on the host from 00:23 to 00:26, ending a
minute before the turn. The maintainer accepted the turn as a host stall, as
0.2.2 did for its host stalls
([#1263](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1263),
[#1340](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1340)), rather
than run the final again. The evidence is in the run's `failures/1/`
directory: the three nodes' journals, their turn histograms and the
verdict.

## A quarter of the job throughput

The final met its target, 1,552.4 accepted jobs a second against 1,390, but
0.2.4's final ran 6,899.6 on the same rig, and the jobs fast tier on this
same candidate ran 6,307.0. The slowdown shows in every job measure:

| Measure | 0.2.4 final | 0.2.5 jobs fast | 0.2.5 final |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accepted a second | 6,899.6 | 6,307.0 | 1,552.4 |
| 1,048,576-task array, median wall time | 197 s | 215 s | 978 s |
| Job-start p99, quiet tasks | 32 ms | 32 ms | 128 ms |

The final's first array took 204 s, the normal time. The second took 807 s
and the third 2,085 s; the remaining 51 took between 844 and 2,593 s, with no
trend over the 10 hours. So the rate fell once, between 21:22 and 21:30 GMT,
and stayed down. The host's power log has the first of three thermal
emergencies at 21:24 to 21:27. The Mac had been plugged in at 21:12 with
10% charge, after a day on battery, and the final started at 21:14 with the
battery charging and three VMs at full load.

A product regression would show in the fast tier, which ran the same binaries
with the same job load and the same faults, compressed, and it didn't; nor
would it start six minutes in and hold flat for ten hours. The 12-hour memory
run after the tag runs the same job load on the published release and shows
whether the rate comes back on a cool host.

## RUSTSEC-2026-0433

[RUSTSEC-2026-0433](https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0433.html)
was published against `pulldown-cmark` 0.13.5, the version in the
candidate's `Cargo.lock`, after the candidate was built. 0.2.5 ships it
(maintainer decision, 2 November 2026), because it isn't exposed: the bug
needs `Options::ENABLE_WIKILINKS`, and the only parser in Reliaburger,
`src/relish/manual/render.rs`, enables tables alone, on the manual's text
compiled into `relish`, never on input from a user or a cluster. `main` has
0.13.6 since [#1528](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/1528),
so 0.2.6 doesn't carry it.

## Apple Container and S3

0.2.5 touches `src/grill/apple.rs` (#1512) and log export
(`src/ketchup/export.rs`, #1509), which by [who runs an ignored
test](../testing.md#who-runs-an-ignored-test) owes `make test-apple` and
`make test-s3`. Neither change reaches what those tests cover:

- **`src/grill/apple.rs`** gained one portable unit test, and nothing else;
  the runtime code is as 0.2.4 shipped it. Direct Apple Container is disabled
  in releases anyway. `make test-apple` ran on the candidate on 3 November
  2026, after the final, with Apple's `container` 0.10.0: three of its four
  tests passed, and
  `apple_serves_a_readonly_bind_mount_with_requested_identity_and_port`
  failed three times out of three, "published port did not serve the bind
  mount". The same container started by hand with `container run`, with no
  Reliaburger involved, couldn't be reached either: not on its published
  port, and not on its own address, where the host had no route to it. That's
  the host's container networking, not the candidate.
- **`src/ketchup/export.rs`** changed how a directory destination is
  written. The S3 path changed only by moving its object name into
  `archive_object_name`, which builds the same `<sha256>-<file>` key as
  before.
