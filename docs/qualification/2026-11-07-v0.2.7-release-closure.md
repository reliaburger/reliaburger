# 0.2.7 release closure

7 November 2026. How the 0.2.7 candidate was qualified: what each gate run
found, and what's left for after the tag. The per-run evidence lives in the
records this page links; this page is the map.

> **Candidate 1 (`ace072c7`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane)
> and the V02 and jobs fast tiers. Both fast tiers were clean, with no
> agent-loop turn over budget. The 8-hour jobs final completed its whole
> schedule: 8 cycles, four upgrade walks and a quiet tail with every leak
> checker armed on every node. It recorded one failure, and every other check
> passed: one `follow_up` turn on node 1 of 1,215 ms, against the agent
> loop's 1 s budget, at 14:04 GMT
> ([#1707](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1707),
> [below](#one-slow-journal-turn-on-node-1)).
>
> The maintainer accepted it on 7 November 2026 and released 0.2.7 with it
> as a known issue, as 0.2.6 did with the same family of failure (#1606).
> That's a decision, not the harness-artefact rule: by the rule of
> [releasing.md](../releasing.md#staging-a-candidate) the final is not clean.
> The fix is tracked in 0.2.8.
>
> **Known issues it ships with:** #1707.

## The gate

0.2.7 needs the full gate ([a gate scaled to the
release](../releasing.md#a-gate-scaled-to-the-release)): `release_tier.py`
found that it changes the agent loop (`src/bun/agent.rs` and 17 files under
`src/bun/agent/`). `scripts/release/nightly.py freeze-check` found no green
nightly to shorten it. The gate was two staged installs, the V02 fast tier,
the jobs fast tier, one 8-hour jobs final and the power-cut and reboot lanes,
25 runs each, all on the candidate itself.

It ran on the Mac Studio, as 0.2.6's did, with nothing else built or tested
on the Studio while it ran. 0.2.8's development ran on a laptop meanwhile.

## One slow journal turn on node 1

At 14:04 GMT, in a quiet slot with no fault active, a heavy check found one
turn on node 1 over the 1 s budget:

```
bun: agent loop turn took 1215 ms in follow_up (consumer_sync/journal): journal write 1215 ms
```

Between 13:55 and 14:08 GMT, discovery journal writes took 1.0 to 1.4 s on all
three nodes. Nearly every consumer step stopped at the turn deadline, about
500 ms, and handed its write to the background ("a discovery journal write
took 1219 ms; the agent loop went on meanwhile"). This one waited for its
whole write inside the turn instead. The likely path is a consumer-step
publication journalled with no deadline
(`journal_discovery_publication(.., None, WriteOwner::ConsumerStep)`, then
`wait_for_landing`, in `src/bun/agent/discovery_ownership.rs`). It's the same
family as 0.2.6's slow compaction turn,
[#1606](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1606), which 0.2.7
fixed for the compaction step. Nodes 2 and 3 had no turn over budget in the
whole run.

The maintainer accepted it on 7 November 2026 rather than build a second
candidate and run the gate again.
[#1707](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/1707) tracks the
fix in 0.2.8. The evidence is in the final's `failures/1/` directory: the
three nodes' journals, diagnostics and the cluster state.
