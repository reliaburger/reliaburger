# 0.3.0 release closure

19 to 20 November 2026. How the 0.3.0 candidate was qualified: what each
gate run found, and what follows from it. The per-run evidence lives in the
records this page links; this page is the map.

> **Candidate 4 (`efc3fb2f`) is the release candidate.** It passed both
> staged installs, the power-cut and reboot lanes (25 of 25 in every lane),
> the jobs fast tier and a second V02 fast tier, all clean. The first V02
> fast tier failed only on a coverage check that a job campaign must overlap
> a power-off (#2105). The 8-hour jobs final completed its whole schedule
> (8 cycles, four upgrade walks, and a quiet tail with every leak checker
> armed on every node, all ok) with one failure: one catalogue pulse's
> `deploy_from_cluster_registry` case hit its 5-minute deadline (#2128).
> No agent-loop turn went over budget in any tier.
>
> **Known issues it ships with:** #2128, accepted by the maintainer on
> 20 November 2026: one stall in 59 runs of the case, right after an injected
> disk fault, which recovered by itself with no data lost. It moves to 0.3.1,
> with better evidence capture for timed-out cases.
>
> **Hardware:** the maintainer's exit test on the Wyse lab ("Bare metal in
> an hour"), which no soak covers, runs on candidate 4's artefacts in
> parallel with the release; the maintainer chose not to hold the tag for it.
> Anything it finds goes into 0.3.1.

## The gate

0.3.0 needs the full gate ([a gate scaled to the
release](../releasing.md#a-gate-scaled-to-the-release)): it moves the wire
format and durable state to protocol 60 and state 76 (#2098). Every tier
ran on the candidate itself: two staged installs, the power-cut and reboot
lanes (25 runs each), the V02 fast tier, the jobs fast tier and the 8-hour
jobs final, then a second V02 fast tier after the first missed its coverage
check. All of it ran on the Mac Studio, with nothing else built or tested
there while it ran.

Candidates 0 to 3 were rehearsals on the train (14 to 17 November), staged
as `nightly-v0.3.0-*` builds to shake out the gate before the freeze; what
they found was fixed on the train before candidate 4 (for example #1965,
from candidate 2's V02 fast tier). Their evidence stays on the Studio.

## The release build

The tag is the merge of the train into main,
[`3a8cff38`](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/commit/3a8cff38bc5898b4b38453bc3e1c4e861c2a7ffd)
([#490](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/pull/490)), not
candidate 4 itself. The two differ only in `build.rs`: seven lines that skip
embedding the source bundle in a fuzzing build (#1953), which a release build
never is. So the soaks, the power-cut lanes and the fast tiers carry over from
candidate 4, and only the staged installs ran again, on the release build.

## What the gate found

**A coverage check that depends on luck ([#2105](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/2105)).**
The V02 fast tier needs a runc job campaign active while a node is powered
off. Seed 798889818 put no power-off inside a campaign, so the check failed
with nothing wrong in the product. The second run, on a fresh seed, covered
it and passed. #2105 makes the schedule guarantee the overlap, in 0.3.1.

**A registry case stalled after a slow fsync ([#2128](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/issues/2128)).**
In the final, the catalogue pulse at 04:17 GMT on 20 November started 28 s
after a 163 s `slow-fsync` fault on node 1 had settled. Its
`deploy_from_cluster_registry` case staged the image and applied the app,
then stalled in the wait for it to run or in the probe of it, and hit its
300 s deadline. Its 58 other runs in the gate evidence passed in 20 s
(median), at most 41.7 s. The cluster recovered by itself: both later pulses
passed, and no data was lost. The cause isn't
verified yet.
