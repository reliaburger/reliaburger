# Archived plans

These are the plans and reviews that took Reliaburger from the first wiring
review in July 2026 to the 0.1.0 release on 29 September 2026. They're kept
as history, not as instructions. Their checkboxes, "not ready" verdicts and
"remaining work" lists describe the code on the day each one was written, and
most of that work has since shipped, been superseded or moved to the current
backlog. Nothing here is edited after it lands, apart from repointing
relative links when files move.

For what actually happened, start with the
[0.1.0 release closure record](../../qualification/2026-09-27-v0.1.0-release-closure.md):
it lists every candidate, soak run and fix from #196 to the tag, and what was
carried past 0.1.0. What's still open lives in [progress.md](../../progress.md)
and in the GitHub milestones 0.1.1 to 0.4.0, not in these files.

Roughly, in order:

- **July: reviews and the Phase 12b programme.** The wiring review
  ([2026-07-02](2026-07-02-review-codebase.md)), the phase plans for 12 to 15
  (optimisations, TUI, self-upgrade, chaos and diagnostics), the reviews after
  Phase 12, and the per-theme plans for 12b.1 to 12b.6.
- **July to August: the current-state and code-logic reviews,** the Phase 15
  follow-up and the post-Phase-15 audits.
- **September: 0.1.0.** The [release-readiness review](2026-09-16-v0.1.0-release-plan.md),
  the [codebase completion plan](2026-09-17-codebase-completion-plan.md)
  (C01 onwards), the per-feature plans of 19 to 22 September, the session
  handoff, the [PR #167 review fixes](2026-09-22-pr167-review-fixes.md), the
  [remaining work](2026-09-22-v0.1.0-remaining-work.md) for the V01 to V04 gates
  and [zero to cluster in five minutes](2026-09-23-zero-to-cluster.md).
