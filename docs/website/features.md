FEATURE STATUS · 0.2.8

# What's ready, and what isn't.

Every feature of Reliaburger 0.2.8, released on 12 November 2026: how far along it is, what it doesn't do yet, and the open issues against it. Search the page for the thing you need.

Before 1.0.0, all of it is for home labs and dev clusters, not production. 1.0.0 is expected in January 2027. Until then, an upgrade can mean starting a fresh cluster.

## What the statuses mean

| Status  | What it means                                                                                |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ready   | Built, documented, tested and exercised by the release gate, with no known serious problems. |
| Beta    | Works and is tested, but has known gaps, limited soak evidence, or may change shape.         |
| Alpha   | Usable to try, but incomplete or likely to change.                                           |
| Planned | Not built yet. The row names its milestone and issue.                                        |

The release gate is what every release passes before it ships: two installs from empty caches, hours of soak under faults with apps and jobs, and power cuts mid-write ([how it works](https://github.com/reliaburger/reliaburger/blob/v0.2.8/docs/releasing.md#the-release-gate)). Unit tests alone don't make a feature Ready.

In 0.2.8, 34 features are Ready, 47 are Beta and 8 are Alpha, 89 in all, and 21 more are planned.
